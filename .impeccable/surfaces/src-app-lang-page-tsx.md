# Home (FR/EN) — Activités Nature

Scope: home page /[lang] plus /[lang]/rafting and /[lang]/canyoning sharing the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: summer tourists around Embrun / Serre-Ponçon, on a phone. Action: send a booking request (WhatsApp or e-mail, pre-filled) or call.
Proof: the client's own 28 photographs; guides' real facts (state diplomas, 30+ years, Baratier). No reviews, no invented stats. Prices are samples to confirm.
Memorable moment: the poster window that opens to the whole screen; the booking ticket that prices the group live.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Hautes-Alpes travel poster becomes the site: flat light colour fields (sky, ridge, Serre-Ponçon lake, Embrun sun) carry every section, and the word DURANCE holds the first screen like a poster title. Refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed video hero over icon cards and testimonials.

OWN-WORLD: Grounds sky #cfe7f0, ridge #8fb9cb, lake #3fb3ac, sun #f5c04a, ink #0d2b3e; night mode re-inks the same poster (navy ground, pale sky ink). One family, Archivo, wide 112–125% black for poster titles, normal width for text. Square corners, flat fills, no shadows, no lines; the ridge silhouette is the only drawn shape. Buttons: solid sun-yellow rectangles, ink text.

STORY: The visitor sees where (Durance, Embrun), what (rafting and canyoning, half-day, from 7), and how much; sees each outing as its own poster with fixed slots (durée, âge, prix); follows the half-day ritual verb by verb, each thrill paired with its safety; meets Xavier and Patrick; prices the group on a ticket and sends the request.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sky ground, nav top (wordmark left, 4 links + yellow Réserver right). h1 at left: DURANCE in wide black at ~10vw with the line "Rafting & canyoning à Embrun" under it. A 4:5 photo window right of centre. Ridge silhouette across the middle, lake band along the bottom holding the lede, three fact slots (2 h 30 · dès 7 ans · dès 45 €) and the primary button. On scroll the window opens to full-bleed.
