# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP (+ ScrollTrigger, @gsap/react) and Lenis for motion, ReactBits components via the shadcn registry. Same stack family as the previous site, deployed on Vercel.

## Users

Summer tourists staying around Embrun and Lac de Serre-Ponçon (Hautes-Alpes), French and foreign, mostly on a phone, often already on holiday and deciding what to do in the next few days. Families with children (7+), groups of friends, bachelor/bachelorette parties, beginners with no white-water experience.

## Product Purpose

Activités Nature is two independent state-certified instructors, Xavier and Patrick, selling half-day rafting on the Durance and canyoning (Le Couleau, Canyon du Fournel). The site exists to get a visitor to send a booking request (activity, date, group size) or call/WhatsApp directly. Success = booking requests received.

## Positioning

Not a big rafting base: two named guides who run every outing themselves, small groups, 30+ years combined experience in the Hautes-Alpes, based at Baratier on the shore of Serre-Ponçon, at the foot of Embrun.

## Operating Context

- Season: 1 April to 2 October, every day.
- Format: half-day, about 2h30 including about 1h30 of practice.
- Rafting on the Durance at Embrun; route adapted to the group; no experience needed.
- Canyoning: Le Couleau (family, from 7 years: walking, small jumps, natural slides, caves); Canyon du Fournel (sporty, from 12 years, Argentière-la-Bessée, 40 min from Embrun: 2–7 m jumps, natural slide, via ferrata passage). Training zone before jumps.
- Equipment provided: wetsuit, life jacket, booties, helmet, harness. Bring: swimsuit, closed shoes, towel, dry clothes.
- Reduced rate from 3 participants.
- Address: Z.A. Les Portes du Lac, 05200 Baratier. Phone +33 7 66 01 05 09. Email activites.nature@gmail.com. Facebook: natureactivites.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Booking: no backend. A request form (activity, date, group size, name) composes a pre-filled email or WhatsApp message to the guides; direct call link always visible.
- Languages: French (default) and English.
- Prices: none confirmed. Sample prices are shown and MUST be confirmed by the client before launch.
- Light-first design, with a dark mode.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Activités Nature". Logo at public/logo.png. Guides' first names Xavier and Patrick. Tone: friendly, safe, convivial ("ambiance conviviale et sécurisée").

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photographs: public/images/rafting/rafting-01..17.jpg, public/images/canyoning/canyoning-01..11.jpg (the client's own outings).
- No testimonials published yet (Facebook reviews exist at facebook.com/natureactivites). Do not invent reviews, ratings, customer counts or press.

## Product Principles

1. Answer the tourist's questions first: what, where, how long, from what age, how much, how to book.
2. The guides are the product: show the two people, not a faceless base.
3. Booking in under a minute from a phone.
4. Safety reassures, fun sells: both are always visible.
